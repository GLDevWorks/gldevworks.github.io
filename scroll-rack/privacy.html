Scroll Rack privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · Applies to Scroll Rack on Android, Windows and Linux

In short: Scroll Rack doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. It has:
  • no accounts;
  • no analytics, tracking or crash reporting;
  • no ads.
Everything you make in the app stays on your device.

What stays on your device

These are saved only on your phone or computer, and GLDevWorks never receives them:

  • your decks, your collection and your settings;
  • the downloaded card database, saved card pictures and tournament results;
  • an optional TopDeck.gg API key, if you choose to add one.

Services the app contacts

To show card and tournament information, the app requests data from these services. They receive what any website receives, your device's IP address, plus what's needed to answer, and their own privacy policies apply. Every request identifies the app as "Scroll Rack", with no information about you.

ServiceWhat it's used forWhat it receives
Scryfall Card data, prices, rulings, card pictures, the daily card database Your card searches and the cards you open
GitHub A public archive of recent tournament decklists Requests for the archive files (no searches)
MTG Bench How often cards appear in casual Commander decks The names of the cards you look up
Commander Spellbook Combos in a deck The card names in the deck you check
TopDeck.gg cEDH tournament results (only if you add your own key) Your key and the formats requested
Wizards of the Coast Downloading the official Comprehensive Rules A request for the rules file

Some buttons open other websites in your browser, for example TCGplayer, EDHREC, MTGTop8 and MTGGoldfish. Once you're on another site, its own privacy policy applies.

Permissions (Android)

  • Internet access: to reach the services above.
  • Network state: to tell Wi-Fi from mobile data, so large downloads wait for Wi-Fi unless you allow mobile data.

The app doesn't use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files. Copying to the clipboard and sharing a deck or collection only happen when you tap those buttons.

Diagnostics

"Copy diagnostics" copies a short technical log (app version and start-up steps) to your clipboard so you can include it in a bug report. It isn't sent anywhere unless you paste it into a message yourself.

Children

Scroll Rack isn't directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage in Android settings) deletes everything it saved. On Windows and Linux, the app's data folders are removed when you delete them or uninstall.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
