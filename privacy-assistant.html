Privacy note: GLDevWorks' Google sign-in

Effective November 19, 2026

This note covers the Google sign-in shown as "GLDevWorks assistant". It isn't an app for the public, and it isn't part of Scroll Rack; Scroll Rack's own policy is here.

What it is

It lets the GLDevWorks developer's own AI assistant, running on the developer's computer, use the GLDevWorks Google account. The assistant drafts and sends GLDevWorks email and keeps app files in GLDevWorks' Google Drive. Only the developer's own account signs in with it.

How data is handled

  • Google account data (email, Drive files) is accessed only for GLDevWorks' own work, at the developer's request.
  • The access permission is stored on the developer's computer and is never shared.
  • When the assistant reads or writes an email or file, that content is processed by the AI model the assistant uses (Anthropic's Claude), under that provider's terms.
  • No Google data is sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models by GLDevWorks.
  • Use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's Third-party connections page.
