Scroll Rack

A free Magic: The Gathering app for Android, Windows and Linux:

  • card search in plain English;
  • card pages with prices, rulings and printings;
  • a deckbuilder with mana-base and draw-odds help;
  • the Comprehensive Rules;
  • tournament usage for formats like Duel Commander and Modern.

No ads, no purchases, no accounts. Your decks and collection stay on your device.

Coming to Google Play and the Microsoft Store. · Privacy policy
